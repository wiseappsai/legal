Privacy Policy — Khatt: Learn Arabic Writing

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Khatt is an app for Android, iPhone and iPad for learning and practising Arabic handwriting, published by WiseAppsAI. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

  • Khatt does not collect, send or share any personal data. The app does not connect to the internet, so it cannot send anything off your device.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

  • Your practice history: scores, streaks, spaced-repetition schedule and mastery.
  • The handwriting you draw in the app, so it can show your first attempt next to your best.
  • Your settings, such as pen style, handedness and strictness.
  • On iPhone and iPad, a summary for the Khatt widgets (streak, today's goal, reviews due and next letter), shared only with the widget on the same device.

All of this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app or clear its data. We have no access to it.

Device backup

If you have turned on device backup (Android backup, or iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad), your device may include Khatt's data in that backup so it can be restored on a new device. This is handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies. We cannot see these backups.

Reminders

On iPhone and iPad you can turn on an optional daily reminder. It is scheduled on your device as a local notification. Nothing is sent to us or to a server.

Text-to-speech

To read letter names and words aloud, Khatt passes the Arabic text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The engine is provided by your device maker or another company, under its own privacy policy. Khatt sends it only the letter or word to be spoken.

Children

Khatt is suitable for learners of every age. It does not ask anyone, including children under 13, for personal information, and it collects none.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
