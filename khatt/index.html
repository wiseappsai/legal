Privacy Policy — Khatt: Learn Arabic Writing

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Khatt is an Android app for learning and practising Arabic handwriting, published by WiseAppsAI. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

  • Khatt does not collect, send or share any personal data. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

  • Your practice history: scores, streaks, spaced-repetition schedule and mastery.
  • The handwriting you draw in the app, so it can show your first attempt next to your best.
  • Your settings, such as pen style, handedness and strictness.

All of this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app or clear its data. We have no access to it.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include Khatt's data in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under Google's privacy policy. We cannot see these backups.

Text-to-speech

To read letter names and words aloud, Khatt passes the Arabic text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The engine is provided by your device maker or another company, under its own privacy policy. Khatt sends it only the letter or word to be spoken.

Children

Khatt is suitable for learners of every age. It does not ask anyone, including children under 13, for personal information, and it collects none.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
