Privacy Policy — Iqra: Quran Memorization

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Iqra is an app for reciting, reviewing and memorizing the Quran, published by WiseAppsAI. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

  • Your recitation is listened to and recognized on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, or to anyone else, while you recite.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.
  • Something leaves your device only if you choose to send a problem report, as described below.

The microphone

When you tap Recite, Iqra uses the microphone so that its on-device speech recognition can follow your recitation on the Mushaf page and point out possible slips. Recognition runs entirely on your iPhone. The microphone is not used at any other time.

Data stored on your device

  • Recordings and recognition logs of your recitation sessions, so you can listen back and review them. You choose how long they are kept, and can delete them, in Settings → Recordings & Diagnostics. They are excluded from iCloud backups.
  • Bookmarks, reading position, reading history and settings.
  • Recitation audio, tafsir and Mushaf fonts that the app downloads for you.

All of this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. We have no access to it.

Problem reports (only if you send one)

The app's recognition can be wrong. For example, it may mark a correct recitation as a slip. If that happens, you may send a report from Report an Issue. Nothing is sent unless you tap Submit. A report contains:

  • the audio recording of that session (your voice),
  • the app's recognition log for that session,
  • the Surah and Ayah, the kind of problem you chose, and any note you write,
  • the app version and iOS version.

A report does not include your name, email, Apple ID, location, contacts or any device identifier. Reports are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, and are used only to find and fix recognition errors. They are never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to identify you.

To have a report deleted, email us with the approximate date and time you sent it and the Surah and Ayah it was about, and we will delete it.

Content downloaded from other sources

To show and play Quran content, the app downloads it from:

  • Recitation audio: everyayah.com
  • Tafsir: api.qurancdn.com (Quran.com) and raw.githubusercontent.com (TarteelAI quran-assets)
  • Mushaf page fonts: verses.quran.foundation (Quran Foundation)

These requests ask only for the content (for example, a Surah and Ayah) and carry no personal information. As with any web request, the provider can see your device's IP address, under its own privacy policy.

Children

Iqra is suitable for reciters of every age and does not ask anyone, including children under 13, for personal information. Because a problem report contains a voice recording, we ask that children send reports only with a parent's permission. A parent may ask us to delete a report at any time.
