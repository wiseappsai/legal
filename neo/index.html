Privacy Policy — NEO: Local AI

Last updated: 31 May 2026

NEO is an offline, on-device AI assistant. It is built so that your text never leaves your phone. This policy explains exactly what does and does not happen with your data.

What we collect

Nothing. NEO does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or the text you write. There are no accounts and no analytics servers receiving your content.

Your text stays on your device

  • All AI processing runs locally on your phone using an on-device model.
  • The text you type, paste, or generate is processed in memory on the device and is not uploaded anywhere.
  • NEO can run with no internet connection at all.

Internet use

NEO only uses the internet for one optional thing: downloading an AI model file (about 0.5 GB) the first time you set it up. These model files are fetched from public model hosts (e.g. Hugging Face). No personal data is sent in these requests beyond what a normal file download requires.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's standard share sheet sends the text you chose to the app you pick (e.g. WhatsApp). That action is initiated by you, and the receiving app's own privacy policy then applies.

Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to download model files.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / FOREGROUND_SERVICE — to show download progress.

Children

NEO does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
